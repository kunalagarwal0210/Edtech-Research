# Validation Survey — EdTech Case Study 4

**Purpose:** convert the ~12-interview qualitative signals into countable evidence, and double as the **task-path picker**. Not a blocker — patch results into the PRD when they land.
**Format:** easy, mobile, ~2 min. Paste into Google Forms.
**Two highest-value fields:** Q5 (free-text task) and Q12 (interview opt-in).
**Written:** 2026-08-18. Context: `edtech-primary-research.md` (Section 7), locked hypothesis v2 (memory).

---

**Intro:** "2-min survey on how professionals learn to use AI at work. No right answers, not selling anything. Helps us build something genuinely useful."

## Screener
1. Your role / function? *(short text)*
2. How technical is your work? — *Not technical at all · Somewhat (use tools, don't code) · Technical / I code*
3. In the last year, have you tried to get better at using AI for your work? — *Yes, actively · Thought about it, did little · No*

## Pain ranking (core)
4. When you've tried to learn AI for work, what stopped you most? *(pick up to 2 / rank top 3)*
   - Didn't know where to start / what to learn first
   - Too much jargon, couldn't follow
   - Learned it but had nowhere to actually apply it
   - Started a course/video but lost momentum and dropped off
   - Couldn't tell which course/tool was actually worth it
   - Too expensive / already spent money and felt burned

## Path-picker (surfaces the single high-intent task)
5. If AI could just *do one thing well for your job tomorrow*, what would it be? *(short text — GOLD, keep free-text)*
6. Which are you most keen to actually *do* with AI? *(pick 1)*
   - Write/draft work content (emails, docs, JDs)
   - Analyze data / summarize long documents
   - Automate a repetitive task
   - Build a simple tool/app
   - Research faster
   - Something specific to my field

## Format confirmation
7. What would you actually stick with? *(pick)* — *10–15 min daily bites · One long weekend session · Live classes · Self-paced long videos*
8. Would a short inline quiz/activity while learning help you? — *Yes / No / Maybe*

## Willingness to pay + trust
9. Have you paid for an AI/tech course before? — *Yes, worth it · Yes, felt burned · No*
10. How would you rather pay for something that genuinely worked? *(pick)* — *Per specific use-case/skill · Monthly subscription · One-time fee · Only if free first, then decide*
11. What monthly price would feel "fair and trustworthy," not scammy? *(short text / ranges)*

## Interview opt-in (recruiting funnel)
12. Open to a 20-min chat to go deeper? Drop your email/phone. *(optional text)*

---

### What each question validates (for the PRD write-up later)
| Q | Validates |
|---|---|
| 2, 3 | Persona fit (non-technical, tech-enabled, tried-and-acted) |
| 4 | Rank the pains — is "where to start" / momentum / jargon the #1? |
| 5, 6 | **Task-path picker** — confirms or challenges the hardcoded "prompting → real-output" task |
| 7, 8 | Format — byte-sized daily + inline quiz demand |
| 9, 10, 11 | WTP shape (per-use-case vs sub) + trustworthy price point |
| 12 | Deeper-interview recruiting |
