# Primary Interview Guide — EdTech Case Study 4

**Purpose:** confirm or KILL the locked hypothesis (non-technical career-switcher, "map + momentum" wedge) before hardening the MVP skill tree.
**Method:** 6–8 behavioral interviews · 20–30 min · past-tense · no pitching.
**Written:** 2026-08-17. Context: `edtech-case-study-handoff.md` + locked hypothesis (project memory).

---

## 1. What these interviews must conclude (6 decisions)

Every question ladders to one of these. If you can't answer these at the end, do more interviews.

| # | Conclude | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Is the pain **real** — shown as *action* (already tried & stalled), not a wish? | #1 open risk. Only prior interview was a *technical* person. No action = pain not real enough. |
| **G2** | When it broke: **map** (didn't know what/where), **momentum** (started, quit), or **external** (time/money/confidence)? | This is the whole wedge. Map+momentum = bet is right. Mostly external = product doesn't fix the real problem. |
| **G3** | Do they think in terms of **one specific target role**? Which recurs? | We hardcode ONE path. Most-mentioned role picks it. |
| **G4** | Did **doing/completing** break, or **consuming**? | Validates the daily *doing*-loop vs "more content." |
| **G5** | What did "making progress" feel like — or its absence? | Feeds progress bar / gamification / retention proxy. |
| **G6** | What have they already **spent** (time + money)? | Real pain spends. Feeds fake-door WTP thesis. |

## 2. Who to recruit

**Primary persona — non-technical switcher:**
- ~26–38, working, **non-technical function** (ops, finance, marketing, HR, sales, support, teaching, content).
- In the **last ~12 months** felt/said they want to move into a more technical / data / AI-adjacent role.
- Took **at least one concrete step** (searched, enrolled, asked someone, started a tutorial).

**3-question screener (before booking):**
1. "In the last year, have you wanted to move into a more tech/data/AI-type role?" (No → not persona)
2. "Did you actually do anything about it — search, sign up, ask someone?" (No → weak, deprioritize)
3. "What's your current role?" (Already technical → exclude from primary)

**Anti-personas (don't pollute the sample):**
- Already-technical (engineers, analysts, the Power BI dev) — optional *contrast group* only, not counted as persona.
- Zero desire to switch (no pain).
- Students with no work context.

**Logistics:** record with consent or take verbatim notes (need exact words for the landing page). Recruit via LinkedIn, cohort, ex-colleagues, communities.

## 3. The guide

**Open (30 sec):** "I'm researching how people try to learn new tech skills for their careers. No right answers, I'm not selling anything — I just want to hear what actually happened. Can I record?"

**Core questions** (listen-for = what you're mining):

1. **"Tell me about the last time you thought about moving into a more technical or data/AI role. What kicked it off?"**
   → real trigger vs none (G1).
2. **"What did you actually do about it? Walk me through it step by step."**
   → real past-tense actions vs "meaning to" (G1).
3. **"When you tried to figure out *what* to learn, what happened?"**
   → "no idea where to start / too many options" = MAP problem (G2).
4. **"Did you have a specific role in mind, or more general?"** → if specific: **"Which one?"**
   → exact target role; do they think in role terms (G3). Tally.
5. **"Of the things you started — courses, videos, tutorials — what happened with each?"**
   → did they finish? where did they drop? = MOMENTUM (G2, G4).
6. **"Think about a time you stopped halfway. What was going on that week?"** *(critical)*
   → external (time/money/life) vs internal (boring, no progress, felt lost). Discriminates the bet (G2).
7. **"Was there ever a moment you felt you were actually getting somewhere? What made it feel that way?"** *(if never: "What would progress have looked like?")*
   → what "progress" means to them (G5).
8. **"How much time or money have you put into this so far?"**
   → real spend = real pain; WTP (G6).

**Closing probe:** "If a friend in your exact situation asked you for advice today, what would you tell them?"

**Rules:**
- NEVER ask "would you use an app that…" (polite answers, worthless).
- Keep them in the **past**.
- On vagueness ("it was hard") ask **"what specifically happened?"**
- Follow the pain, not the hypothesis. If the real break is money/confidence, that's a finding.

## 4. How to know you're done (synthesis test)

Fill in with evidence behind every blank:

> "*[non-technical persona]* wanted to move into *[specific role]* but couldn't, because *[map / momentum / external]*; they tried *[X]*, which failed because *[reason]*."

**Confirmation / kill criteria:**
- **CONFIRMED** if ~4+ took *real action* and stalled on **map or momentum** → build proceeds as locked.
- **Wedge in trouble** if most breaks are **external** (no time/money/confidence) → daily-loop doesn't fix their blocker; rethink before building.
- **Path picked:** most-mentioned **target role** (Q4) → the one hardcoded skill tree.
- **Doing-loop validated** if people *start and abandon* rather than *never find content* → doing > consuming.
